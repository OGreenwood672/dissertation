    \multicolumn{3}{|c|}{\textbf{Core Reinforcement Learning Concepts}} \\
    \hline
    RL & Reinforcement Learning & A machine learning paradigm where agents learn to make sequential decisions by taking actions in an environment to maximise cumulative reward. \\
    \hline
    MARL & Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning & A subfield of reinforcement learning involving multiple agents situated in a shared environment, learning to maximise rewards through interaction. \cite{Overview, SurveyofMARL} \\
    \hline
    MDP & Markov Decision Process & A mathematical framework for modelling decision making in situations where outcomes are partly random and partly under the control of a decision maker. \\
    \hline
    POMDP & Partially Observable Markov Decision Process & An extension of an MDP where the agent cannot directly observe the underlying state of the environment, requiring inference or communication. \cite{POMDP-COM} \\
    \hline
    Dec-POMDP & Decentralised Partially Observable Markov Decision Process & A cooperative game framework where multiple agents act on the same state, receive individual local observations, and share a common reward signal. \\
    \hline
    CTDE & Centralised Training Decentralised Execution & A MARL paradigm where agents are trained using a centralised critic with access to global state information, but act independently using only local observations during execution. \\
    \hline

    \multicolumn{3}{|c|}{\textbf{Reinforcement Learning Algorithms \& Mechanisms}} \\
    \hline
    VDN & Value-Decomposition Networks & A MARL architecture that assumes the joint action-value function can be additively decomposed into individual agents' value functions. \cite{VDN} \\
    \hline
    TRPO & Trust Region Policy Optimisation & A predecessor to PPO that updates policies by taking a step that improves performance while maintaining a strict region of trust. \cite{TRPO} \\
    \hline
    PPO & Proximal Policy Optimisation & A policy gradient method for reinforcement learning that utilises a clipped surrogate objective function to ensure stable and conservative policy updates. \cite{PPO, ppo_bestpractices} \\
    \hline
    MAPPO & Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimisation & An extension of PPO tailored for multi-agent settings, utilising a shared actor and a centralised critic to mitigate non-stationarity and credit assignment problems. \cite{MAPPO} \\
    \hline
    MADDPG & Multi-Agent Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient & An off-policy actor-critic algorithm for MARL, noted in the text as unsuitable for emergent communication due to semantic shifts during training. \cite{MADDPG} \\
    \hline
    GAE & Generalised Advantage Estimation & An algorithm used to calculate the advantage function in reinforcement learning, balancing the bias-variance trade-off by exponentially discounting future temporal difference errors. \cite{GAE, gae_normalisation} \\
    \hline

    \multicolumn{3}{|c|}{\textbf{Autoencoders \& Latent Representation Models}} \\
    \hline
    VAE & Variational Autoencoder & A generative model architecture consisting of an encoder and a decoder, used to learn compressed, latent representations of data. \\
    \hline
    VQ-VAE & Vector-Quantised Variational Autoencoder & An autoencoder that maps continuous latent vectors to the nearest discrete symbol in a learned finite codebook, acting as a semantic bottleneck. \cite{VQVAE} \\
    \hline
    SQ-VAE & Stochastically-Quantised Variational Autoencoder & A VAE variant using a variational Bayes framework that applies a categorical probability distribution over the codebook to mitigate codebook collapse. \cite{SQVAE} \\
    \hline
    vMF SQ-VAE & von Mises-Fisher Stochastically-Quantised Variational Autoencoder & An extension of SQ-VAE that utilises cosine similarity and normalizes embeddings onto a unit sphere, better suited for semantic feature representation. \cite{SQVAE, semantic-direction} \\
    \hline
    HQ-VAE & Hierarchically-Quantised Variational Autoencoder & A VAE variant that splits representations into hierarchical levels to capture macro-level and micro-level strategies, reducing computational burden. \cite{HQVAE} \\
    \hline

    \multicolumn{3}{|c|}{\textbf{Multi-Agent Communication Protocols}} \\
    \hline
    DIAL & Differentiable Inter-Agent Learning & A foundational multi-agent communication protocol that enables continuous gradient flow between agents during training. \cite{dial-rial} \\
    \hline
    TarMAC & Targeted Multi-Agent Communication & A multi-agent communication framework that employs attention mechanisms to determine which agents to send continuous message vectors to. \cite{tarmac} \\
    \hline

    \multicolumn{3}{|c|}{\textbf{Neural Networks \& Mathematical Components}} \\
    \hline
    MLP & Multi-Layer Perceptron & A foundational feedforward artificial neural network architecture used to process inputs in actor and critic networks. \\
    \hline
    LSTM & Long Short-Term Memory & A type of recurrent neural network module used to process sequences of data, enabling agents to retain allocentric and spatial memory of environments. \\
    \hline
    STE & Straight-Through Estimator & A mathematical technique used to bypass non-differentiable operations (like argmin) by copying gradients directly from output to input during backpropagation. \\
    \hline
    PCA & Principal Component Analysis & A dimensionality reduction technique used to project and visualise the latent language spaces and codebook embeddings. \\
    \hline
    MSE & Mean Squared Error & A standard loss function that measures the average squared difference between a network's predicted values and the actual target values. \\
    \hline
    BCE & Binary Cross-Entropy & A standard loss function used for binary classification or evaluating binary features during neural network training. \\
